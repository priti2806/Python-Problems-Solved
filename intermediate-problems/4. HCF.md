find the hcf of two numbers -

num1 = int(input("Enter first number: "))
num2 = int(input("Enter second number: "))

while num2 != 0:
    num1, num2 = num2, num1 % num2

print("HCF =", num1)

Explanatipn-  HCF means Highest Common Factor — the largest number that divides both numbers exactly.

For example:

12 and 18

Factors of 12 → 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
Factors of 18 → 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

Common factors → 1, 2, 3, 6

num1, num2 = num2, num1 % num2

This uses the Euclidean Algorithm.

Suppose:

num1 = 12
num2 = 18
First iteration:
12 % 18 = 12

So:

num1 = 18
num2 = 12
Second iteration:
18 % 12 = 6

So:

num1 = 12
num2 = 6
Third iteration:
12 % 6 = 0

So:

num1 = 6
num2 = 0

Now:

while num2 != 0

becomes false, so the loop stops.

Therefore:

print("HCF =", num1)

prints:

HCF = 6
Easy way to remember

The algorithm repeatedly does:

larger number ÷ smaller number → take remainder

Then:

smaller number becomes larger → remainder becomes smaller

When remainder becomes 0, the other number is the HCF.
